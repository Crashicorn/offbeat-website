What gets the fastest reply

CorrectionsInclude the page URL, the line that is wrong, and the source that contradicts it. Corrections with a source are usually live within a couple of days.
Gear and software questionsTell us your budget, what you already own, and what you want to do with it. We answer what we can, and questions that come up repeatedly become guides.
AccessibilityTell us the page, the device or assistive technology, and what happened. These reports are treated as bugs, not feedback.
Press and partnershipsBrand and agency enquiries are welcome, with one condition: editorial conclusions are not for sale. Review samples do not come with approval rights.

What we do not respond to

Unsolicited guest posts, link-exchange requests, paid-placement offers and "add my client to your list" pitches go unanswered. Our recommendations are editorial, and the editorial standards explain how they are reached.
