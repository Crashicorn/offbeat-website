Seven tools you can use before you spend anything

Most buying advice is a list. These are the things a list cannot do: price a whole rig, let you hear whether two kicks are actually locked, and tell you which licence a controller really unlocks. All of them run in this browser.

How they work: every tool runs on your own device. Nothing you type or load is uploaded, and the only measurement on the site stays the consent-gated analytics described in the privacy policy.

These tools exist because two questions come up on almost every page: what does this actually cost once I have everything I need, and how do I know when it sounds right. Neither is answered well by a screenshot of a price tag. Where a tool has a limit, the page says what the limit is instead of pretending it is a verdict.

The tools

7 tools
01 · Plan the rig

Rig planner

Price the whole setup, not one box.

Start from a controller and build outward: headphones, monitors, stands and the cables nobody remembers to budget for. Every line is priced, priced-from a named source, and the total moves as you change your mind. You can mark what you already own so it drops out of the bill.

Time
3–5 minutes
Needs
Nothing to install
Cost
Free

Prices are dated October 2026 estimates, not live quotes. Each line links to the product page it was checked against.

Open the rig planner → How the rig is assembled →
02 · Practise by ear

Beatmatching trainer

Hear what beatmatched actually sounds like.

Deck A sits locked at 124 BPM. Deck B arrives slightly out of tempo and out of position, the way a record does when you drop it in. Match it with the pitch fader, then nudge the kicks into phase with the jog control. The drill measures how far apart the kicks were, which is the part your ears cannot yet judge.

Time
45 seconds per run
Needs
Sound on, headphones useful
Cost
Free

Both decks are synthesised live in the browser. Nothing is downloaded, recorded or uploaded.

Start the drill → Beatmatch manually in five steps →
03 · Check compatibility

Controller–software matrix

Find out what a controller actually unlocks.

Filter controllers by software, channel count and budget, then read the licence and connection detail behind each row. A matching app name is not the same as a free licence: the edition, the operating system and the audio outputs all change the answer.

Time
2 minutes
Needs
The model you are considering
Cost
Free

Unlock claims follow the manufacturers' published hardware pages; confirm the current licence terms before you buy.

Open the matrix → Which controllers unlock which software →
04 · Choose software

Software advisor

Seven questions, one platform shortlist.

Answer what you already own, what will run the software and where your music comes from. You get the platforms that fit, the ones that would need a licence you were not expecting, and the ones that will not run at all — each with its three-year cost.

Time
2 minutes
Needs
Your current gear
Cost
Free

Three-year costs are dated October 2026 and link to the vendor's own pricing page.

Open the advisor → Serato vs rekordbox vs Traktor →
05 · Choose speakers

Speaker finder

Match the cabinet to the room, not the wattage.

Pick the job your speakers have to do — desk practice, a shared production desk, a room full of people, or nothing yet. You get a starting point with the cable run it needs, and a straight answer when headphones are the better buy.

Time
30 seconds
Needs
Nothing
Cost
Free

Recommendations are editorial starting points, not sponsored placements.

Open the finder → Monitor speakers compared for rooms →
06 · Choose headphones

Headphone fit finder

Buy the pair that fits your head and your gig.

Clamp force, isolation and replaceable parts matter more than a spec sheet once you are three hours into a set. Say how you will use the headphones and the guide narrows to the pairs that suit that job, with the compromise for each one stated plainly.

Time
30 seconds
Needs
Nothing
Cost
Free

Fit guidance describes the headphone type, not a measurement of your own head.

Open the fit finder → DJ headphones compared for isolation →
07 · Mix in key

Harmonic mixing wheel

Find the keys that mix without clashing.

Pick the key of the track playing and the wheel shows which of the Camelot neighbours will blend, which will lift the energy and which will fight it. You can hear the chord for each key before you trust the number.

Time
1 minute
Needs
Sound on
Cost
Free

Camelot codes describe key relationships; they cannot tell you whether the tracks belong in the same set.

What these tools will not do

They will not tell you a product sounds good, score a controller out of ten, or promise a price that will still be true next month. Prices are dated snapshots with a named source; licence and compatibility statements come from the manufacturers' own pages. If a tool cannot answer something, it says so.

The full method — what is researched, what is not, and how corrections work — is in the editorial standards. Buying links are explained in the affiliate disclosure, and the tools themselves contain none.
